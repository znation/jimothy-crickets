## Name

**Jimothy Crickets**

## Short description

- Google Play (80 characters max): *Help Jimothy the raccoon and his cricket friends win back the garbage pile!* (75)
- App Store subtitle (30 max): *A cozy reverse tower defense* (28)
- Steam / itch.io tagline: *A cute reverse tower defense starring Jimothy, Seattle's roundest raccoon.*

## Long description

> The humans keep the good garbage behind sprinklers, fences and one very confused neighbor with
> a broom. Jimothy has decided this is unacceptable.
>
> Jimothy Crickets turns tower defense around: the towers are the humans', and you send the
> troupe. Crickets swarm, possums play dead, squirrels sprint, crows fly over fences and rats chew
> through them. Pick who goes, when, and down which path, then cheer them on.
>
> - 20 short levels across an alley, back yards, a cul-de-sac and a strip-mall dumpster lot
> - 5 friends to recruit and 7 upgrades to earn with acorns
> - Nobody ever gets hurt: shooed friends just hop off home. There's no losing. If the sun comes
>   up, the troupe takes a nap and tries again tomorrow night.
> - Plays with touch, mouse, keyboard or gamepad (Steam Deck)
> - Free. No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no data collected. Plays offline.
>
> Inspired by Jimothy, the real Seattle raccoon with short spine syndrome, drawn with affection.
