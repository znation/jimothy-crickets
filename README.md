Jimothy Crickets

A tower defense game inspired by our Seattle-local friend Jimothy, the famous raccoon with short spine syndrome. You play as Jimothy, defending your garbage pile from the invading crickets.

The style of the game is cute, friendly, all-ages, simple (even simplistic), approachable. The goal is to highlight our friend Jimothy while providing mildly strategic entertainment in the form of a typical tower defense game. The "twist" here is not in the gameplay or the intricacy, but in the design, Jimothy vs. crickets concept, and simplicity while still providing entertaining gameplay.
