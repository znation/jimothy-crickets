# Audio sources

Every recording here is **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication): no attribution is required, but
we credit the authors anyway. `src/` holds the files as downloaded (renamed; one WAV converted to
Ogg). `tools/build_audio.py` normalizes and encodes them into `public/audio/`.

The animals' voices (cricket, possum, squirrel, crow, rat), the sprinkler and the yard dog are still
synthesized in `src/platform/audio.ts`: no CC0 pack had them in a cute enough register.
